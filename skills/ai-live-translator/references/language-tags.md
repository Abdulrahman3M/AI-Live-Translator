# Language identifiers

Examples of BCP 47 language tags: Arabic `ar` (Egyptian Arabic `ar-EG`), English `en` (`en-GB`, `en-US`), Russian `ru`, French `fr`, Spanish `es`, German `de`, Persian `fa`, Turkish `tr`, Urdu `ur`, Hindi `hi`, Uzbek `uz`, Tajik `tg`, Bengali `bn`, Chinese `zh`, Japanese `ja`, Korean `ko`.

BCP 47 tags are metadata for fixtures and setup; they do not guarantee the chosen model's fluency. The project supports arbitrary requested pairs as a prompt protocol, while its curated sample coverage is limited and stated in corpus stats. Dialect adaptation follows user context.
